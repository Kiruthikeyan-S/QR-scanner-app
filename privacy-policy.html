Privacy Policy for Qrinova

Effective Date: October 21, 2026
App Name: Qrinova
Package Name: com.kiruthikeyan.scanner
Developer: Kiruthikeyan S

1. Introduction

Welcome to Qrinova. Qrinova is a privacy-first QR code and barcode scanner, generator, and URL safety verification app. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

2. Permissions We Request & Why

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time. Camera frames are processed locally on your device and are never recorded or transmitted.
  • Photos / Media / Files: Used only when you explicitly choose an image from your gallery to scan a QR code, or when you select a photo/PDF to generate a shareable Photo QR or File QR code.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Used to check scanned URLs for phishing/safety threats and to upload user-selected photos/PDFs when generating a cloud-hosted Photo/File QR code.

3. Local Storage

Your scan history, saved QR codes, and app settings are stored locally on your device. You can delete your history at any time inside the app or by uninstalling the application.

4. User-Uploaded Files (Photo QR & PDF QR)

When you voluntarily use the Photo QR or PDF QR generator, the selected file is uploaded over HTTPS to our cloud storage (Supabase) so that anyone scanning your generated QR code can view the file via its unique link.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, and we do not include third-party advertising SDKs.

6. Contact Us

For questions or file deletion requests, please contact us via https://github.com/Kiruthikeyan-S/QR-scanner-app.
